This website does not use cookies, tracking or analytics tools.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit the site, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in server logs. Details can be found in the <a href="https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement">GitHub privacy statement</a>.

One demo video is embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). No data are sent to YouTube until you start the video; on playback, YouTube (Google) receives technical data such as your IP address. Details can be found in the <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google privacy policy</a>.

If you contact us by email or via the contact form, we process the data you provide (such as your name, email address and message) only to handle your request. Messages sent via the contact form are forwarded to our mailbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).
